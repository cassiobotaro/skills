An ADR holds one decision, so these four questions will probably become separate records as they get settled. They are also different kinds of question. Ownership is organizational rather than architectural.

To get started, please answer these questions:

1. **Which decision do you want recorded first?** Pick one that has been settled, or one that is close. For example: "we will put a message queue (which one?) between the API and the channel senders".

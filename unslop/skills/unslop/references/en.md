# English vocabulary

Read this file when the text is in English. It continues the rule list of `SKILL.md` and
its ids are stable in the same way.

A listed word is a hit when it carries no information. Leave it when it is the literal
term: a `robust` estimator in statistics, the `landscape` orientation of a page, a
function that `features` in a call graph.

- **EN1.** Stock vocabulary: additionally, comprehensive, crucial, delve, enhance, foster,
  landscape, pivotal, robust, seamless, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant. Use the
  specific word or cut the sentence.
- **EN2.** Say "is" or "has". Not "serves as", "stands as", "acts as", "boasts", "features".
- **EN3.** Filler. "In order to" becomes "to". Delete "it is important to note that",
  "it is worth noting that", "at its core", "when it comes to", "in today's world".
- **EN4.** The plain word: "use", not "utilize" or "leverage". "Help", not "facilitate".
  "Start", not "commence". "About", not "regarding".
- **EN5.** Abstract metaphor nouns: flywheel, harness, landscape, nexus, north star,
  paradigm, primitive, ratchet, scaffolding, substrate, surface, vector, wedge. Use the
  concrete word. A noun on this list stays when it names a real thing in the text, such as
  a test harness.
- **EN6.** Chatbot phrases: "Great question", "Certainly", "I hope this helps", "Let me
  know if", "Feel free to".
- **EN7.** Stacked hedges: "may potentially", "could possibly", "might perhaps". One hedge,
  and "may" is enough.
- **EN8.** Signposts and stock closings: "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "In
  conclusion", "Overall", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day".

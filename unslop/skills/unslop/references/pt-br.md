# Brazilian Portuguese vocabulary

Read this file when the text is in Brazilian Portuguese. It continues the rule list of
`SKILL.md` and its ids are stable in the same way. The examples stay in Portuguese because
they are the strings to look for.

A listed word is a hit when it carries no information. Leave it when it is the literal
term: "cenário de teste", "estimador robusto", "papel" as a role in an access model. An
established technical loanword (deploy, request, cache, commit) is not a hit.

- **PT1.** Stock vocabulary: abrangente, alavancar, aprimorar, crucial, desvendar,
  dinâmico, fomentar, fundamental, impulsionar, inovador, jornada, mergulhar, panorama,
  potencializar, robusto. Use the specific word or cut the sentence.
- **PT2.** Say "é", "tem", or the verb that names the action. Not "serve como", "atua
  como", "conta com", "desempenha um papel em".
- **PT3.** Filler. "A fim de" and "com o objetivo de" become "para". Delete "é importante
  notar que", "vale ressaltar que", "vale destacar que", "cabe lembrar que", "no que diz
  respeito a", "no contexto de". "De forma eficiente" becomes the adverb, or goes.
- **PT4.** The plain word: "usar", not "utilizar". "Ter", not "possuir". The verb, not
  "realizar" or "efetuar" plus a noun ("realizar a validação" becomes "validar").
- **PT5.** No gerundismo: "vai estar enviando" becomes "vai enviar" or "enviará".
- **PT6.** Chatbot phrases: "Ótima pergunta", "Claro!", "Com certeza!", "Espero que isso
  ajude", "Fico à disposição".
- **PT7.** Signposts and stock closings: "Vamos mergulhar", "Em resumo", "Em suma", "Em
  última análise", "Por fim, mas não menos importante", "No mundo atual", "Nos dias de
  hoje".
- **PT8.** Calques of ordinary English words: "endereçar um problema" becomes "tratar" or
  "resolver", "no final do dia" as a conclusion goes, "performar" becomes "ter
  desempenho" or the specific verb, "aplicar para" becomes "candidatar-se a".
- **PT9.** The agentless passive hides the actor in both of its forms: "foi definido",
  "é enviado", "são rotacionados", and "definiu-se", "envia-se". Rule S17 applies.
